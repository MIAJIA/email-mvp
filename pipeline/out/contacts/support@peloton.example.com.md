# Peloton Member Support

Peloton's member support team. Mia has written to them about three subscriptions (2025–2026), each time to cancel the membership and push for a full refund of charges for months she didn't use.

- Address (default): Peloton Member Support (4/7 emails)
- In February 2025 Mia cancelled an App One membership. It began as a free trial in October 2024 and then billed $12.99 a month. Support refunded only the February 18 charge, so she asked for a review of the other $38.97.
- Mia's last class on that first App One membership was on October 27, 2024, during the trial.
- On November 2, 2025, Mia asked to cancel her All-Access Membership ($44.00/month) through the in-app chat, but she was still charged on December 2, 2025 and January 2, 2026.
- Support refunded the January 2 charge. Mia still has the chat transcript showing the cancellation and offered to send it to get the December charge refunded too.
- Her App One annual membership renewed on September 27, 2026 for $119.00 without a reminder. Her last class before that was on March 15, 2026.
- Support first gave a prorated refund of $109.08. Mia asked for a review of the remaining $9.92. When support asked where to send that refund, she confirmed the same payment method.
- Mia always asks for refunds to go to the original payment method, not as account credits.
- Her first email always asks support to confirm three things: the cancellation, that no more charges will be made, and the total refund.
- If support refunds only part of the money, Mia thanks them for that part, then asks for the rest to be reviewed or escalated as a one-time exception.
- Mia includes the email address on her Peloton account at the bottom of each new request.
