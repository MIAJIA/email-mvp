# Notion Support

Notion's support and billing team. Mia is a Notion customer who writes to them to cancel plans and ask for refunds, and she escalates when a refund is only partial.

- Address (formal): Notion Support (4/7 emails)
- This is Notion's customer support team. Mia is a Notion customer and writes to them only about billing and refunds.
- Mia sometimes writes to the Notion Billing Team instead, usually as a reply to a billing email such as a renewal notice or an invoice.
- Mia's annual Notion Plus plan renewed on November 18, 2024 for $96.00. She asked to cancel and downgrade to Free on November 19, 2024.
- In 2024 Mia was the only member of the workspace. Her last edit was on February 11, 2024, and she said she got no renewal reminder.
- For the 2024 renewal, Notion refunded $48.00 (prorated). Mia asked for the remaining $48.00 and asked them to escalate to the billing team or a supervisor.
- Mia wants refunds paid back to her original payment method, not as account credits.
- From May 2025 Mia was charged $10.00 a month for the Notion AI add-on after she tried it once in an April free trial. That came to seven charges, $70.00 in total. She removed the add-on on November 11, 2025.
- Notion refunded the October and November 2025 AI charges. Mia asked for the other five charges (May 5 to September 5, 2025, $50.00) and said her usage log shows no AI requests after the trial.
- In 2026 Mia was on a monthly Plus plan. The September 5, 2026 invoice charged her for two members at $12.00 each. She says she removed the second person, a guest, on August 30, 2026, so she asked for $12.00 back.
- When she follows up, Mia usually thanks them for what was already done, restates the dates, and asks for 'a one-time exception' or for escalation, or at least 'the maximum amount permitted'.
- In follow-up emails Mia adds an 'account email:' line below her name.
- Mia asks them to confirm in writing that the cancellation or removal went through and how much in total is being refunded.
